Alex Grey

DJ · POP / House / Nu Disco / Funky

With over ten years behind the decks, Alex Grey has built a reputation for reading a room and shaping the night around it — whether that's a wedding reception, a corporate function, a private celebration, or a large-scale sporting event. From quiet, warm moments to a dance floor that doesn't stop, every set is built around the crowd in front of him and the brief from the people who hired him.

Based on the Gold Coast, Alex works directly with couples and event organisers before the day to understand the room, the timeline, and the mood they're after — then delivers it live, adjusting on the fly as the night unfolds.
